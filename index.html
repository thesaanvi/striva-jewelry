Heritage & Vision

The Striva Story

Founded on the belief that luxury across India should be inclusive, sustainable, and worn every single day.

Crafted Without Compromise

Striva was born when our founders grew tired of fashion jewelry that tarnished after two monsoon seasons or cost a fortune at traditional family jewelers. We set out to engineer demi-fine jewelry featuring aerospace-grade tarnish resistance, 18k solid gold vermeil, and conflict-free lab gems.

Today, Striva empowers hundreds of thousands of modern individuals across Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and beyond to express their authentic style without environmental guilt or financial strain.
