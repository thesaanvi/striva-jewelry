The Striva Philosophy

What is Demi-Fine Jewelry & How Are We Different?

Traditional fashion jewelry tarnishes within weeks, while solid gold jewelry costs a small fortune. Demi-fine is the golden middle ground.

18k Gold Vermeil & Sterling Silver

We use a thick layer of solid 18k gold (at least 2.5 microns) securely bonded over recycled 925 sterling silver, giving you the weight, look, and durability of solid gold.

Ethical & Conflict-Free

All our gemstones are premium lab-grown diamonds or ethically sourced cubic zirconia, completely free from environmental degradation and human exploitation.

Luxury Without Markup

By selling directly to you online, we eliminate traditional multi-layered retail markups, pricing exquisite pieces fairly between ₹2,500 and ₹10,000.
