What is Demi-Fine Jewelry?

Bridging the gap between fragile fashion jewelry and exorbitant solid gold. We offer robust recycled 925 silver cores, thick 18k gold vermeil, and conflict-free lab-grown gemstones for daily wear without compromise.

Recycled 925 Sterling Silver

Our solid foundation is built entirely on certified recycled 925 silver, ensuring lifetime durability, hypoallergenic comfort, and substantial weight.

Thick 18K Gold Vermeil

Plated up to 5x thicker than standard gold plating using ethical 18K solid yellow and rose gold over sterling silver for enduring luster and warmth.

Ethical Lab-Grown Stones

Adorned with conflict-free lab diamonds, brilliant CZ, pearls, and hand-cut semiprecious gemstones for maximum fire and sustainability.
