The Philosophy of Striva

What is Demi-Fine Jewelry?

High fashion jewelry often forces a compromise: fragile fashion pieces that fade in weeks, or exorbitant fine jewelry reserved for special occasions. Striva bridges this gap.

Substantial Gold Vermeil

We plate a thick, heavy layer of genuine 18k solid gold (at least 2.5 microns) over certified recycled 925 sterling silver, ensuring decades of radiant wear.

Ethically Crafted

Every gemstone is conflict-free and lab-grown or ethically mined. Our artisan workshops ensure fair wages, safe conditions, and zero harmful waste.

Direct-to-You Value

By cutting out traditional luxury middlemen and retail markups, we deliver heirloom-grade craftsmanship at a fraction of traditional boutique costs.

Unmatched Distinction

Designed for the modern muse who refuses to compromise.

Traditional jewelry is either mass-produced mystery metal that turns your skin green, or bank-breaking heirlooms you are afraid to wear out. Striva provides waterproof, sweat-resistant, hypoallergenic pieces you can wear in the shower, at the gym, and at the gala.

Heritage & Vision

The Striva Story

Founded on the belief that luxury should be inclusive, sustainable, and worn every single day.

Crafted Without Compromise

Striva was born in 2022 when our founders grew tired of jewelry that tarnished after two trips to the beach or cost a month's rent. We set out to create a new category: demi-fine jewelry engineered with aerospace-grade tarnish resistance, 18k solid gold vermeil, and conflict-free lab gems.

Today, Striva empowers hundreds of thousands of individuals worldwide to express their authentic style without environmental guilt or financial strain.
